📊 HR Attrition & Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)
Overview
This project showcases an interactive HR Attrition & Workforce Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI with employee attrition data. The dashboard helps analyze workforce demographics, evaluate employee turnover, monitor job satisfaction, and explore the impact of overtime and commuting distance through interactive visualizations.
Objectives
Analyze overall headcount and attrition metrics across organizational departments.
Evaluate turnover rates across different job roles and tenure bands.
Monitor workforce demographics including age distribution, gender ratio, and education fields.
Analyze the impact of burnout indicators like overtime hours and business travel frequency.
Explore employee job satisfaction and work-life balance levels against company benchmarks.
Develop an interactive report to support data-driven talent management and retention strategies.

Business Insights
The dashboard provides an intuitive, executive-ready view of workforce retention and diversity metrics. Based on the analysis, the following insights were observed:
The organization maintains a total headcount of 1,470 employees, with 1,233 active staff and 237 resignations, resulting in an overall attrition rate of 16.1%.
Sales and Research & Development departments account for the highest volume of resignations, with specific roles like Sales Executive and Laboratory Technician exhibiting high flight risk.
Mandatory OverTime is a primary driver of turnover; employees working overtime display a significantly higher percentage of resignations compared to non-overtime staff.
Tenure analysis shows that turnover peaks during the first 1 to 3 years of employment, highlighting crucial early-career retention windows.
Employees with longer commutes (> 15 miles) show elevated turnover rates, pointing to potential work-life balance friction.
Average Job Satisfaction sits at 2.72 out of 4.0, indicating opportunities to improve workplace engagement in key departments.
Interactive slicers allow HR managers to seamlessly filter data by Department, OverTime status, and Performance Rating for targeted analysis.
Key Findings
Total Workforce Headcount reached 1,470, with an overall attrition rate of 16.1%.
High turnover is heavily concentrated in early-tenure stages (1–3 years at company).
OverTime is strongly correlated with increased employee departure rates.
Sales Representatives and Laboratory Technicians represent the highest flight-risk job roles.
Gender diversity stands at approximately 40% female representation across departments.
Commute distances over 15 miles correlate with higher resignation counts.
Weather and non-work demographic factors show minimal direct influence compared to workload and compensation.
Interactive filtering enables HR leaders to quickly isolate risk groups and plan targeted retention programs.
