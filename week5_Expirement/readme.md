# 📊 Retail Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview

This project showcases an interactive **Retail Sales Performance Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** with the Walmart Sales Dataset. The dashboard helps analyze weekly sales, compare store performance, monitor sales trends, and explore the impact of holidays and other business factors through interactive visualizations.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze weekly sales performance across Walmart stores.
- Compare sales performance between different stores.
- Monitor yearly and monthly sales trends.
- Analyze holiday and non-holiday sales.
- Explore the relationship between temperature and weekly sales.
- Develop an interactive dashboard to support business decision-making.

---

## Business Insights

The dashboard provides a simple and interactive view of Walmart's sales performance. Based on the analysis, the following insights were observed:

- Walmart generated approximately **6.74 Billion** in weekly sales during the selected period.
- The dataset contains sales information for **45 stores**.
- Sales reached their highest level in **2011**, followed by a slight decline in 2012.
- Monthly sales remained fairly consistent, with higher sales observed during **April, July, and August**.
- Most of the sales were generated during **non-holiday weeks**, showing stable customer demand throughout the year.
- Sales performance differed across stores, with some stores consistently achieving higher sales than others.
- Temperature showed only a weak relationship with weekly sales, indicating that weather had limited impact on customer purchases.
- Interactive filters allow users to explore sales data by different dates and stores for better analysis.

## Key Findings
